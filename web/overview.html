SYSTEM

From speech to an answer

Microphone / paced WAV

↓ Local Whisper speech recognition

↓ Conversation state + approved knowledge base

↓ Cited answer + localized voice

↘ Live signal rules → alert panel

CHOICES

Why this design?

Small approved dataset → inspectable lexical search.

Exact source answers → fewer invented facts.

Explicit state → confirm missing and changed details.

Rule-based alerts → clear reasons and low processing cost.

Separate market settings → no surprise language switch.

MEASUREMENT

What the evidence shows

Real ASR on synthetic calls.

Four-second chunks replayed at normal speed.

ASR, signal, delivery and total time measured.

Failures stay visible in transcripts and reports.

Text-only tests are labelled separately.

LIMITS

Before a real launch

Approved business rules and consent review.

Native speakers and real regional-accent recordings.

Stronger speech model and noisy phone-audio tests.

Authentication, shared session storage and bounded queues.
